How it helps

One choice,
real carbon saved.

EcoCart turns makers' reports and energy labels into a difference you can see before you pay. Three real examples from Amazon.in, with the figures EcoCart shows on the page.

8 kg CO₂e less

Choosing the iPhone Air over the iPhone 18 Pro, by Apple's own reports for the 256GB models, and ₹15,000 cheaper too. About the same as 57 km in a petrol car.

27 kg CO₂e a year

Choosing the Whirlpool 235 L fridge over the Haier 237 L: their labels say 38 kWh a year less, on India's grid. At ₹8 a unit that is ₹304 off the bill, every year it runs.

2031

Apple promises security updates for the iPhone 18 Pro until at least September 2031. A phone that stays safe to use can be used for longer, and most of its footprint comes from making it.

Figures checked on 11 October 2026 from Apple's environmental reports, the listings' BEE labels, the Central Electricity Authority's grid factor and India GHG Program's car factor. They describe these products, not every choice. Try both comparisons in the demo.

When EcoCart helps

Your question.
A clear next step.

EcoCart is for the moment you are deciding, while the greener choice is still yours to make. Pick the question you have.

Choose the phone that lasts longer and costs the planet less.

  1. Know. How long it gets security updates, and the lifecycle footprint its maker reports, each with the date it was checked.
  2. Check. If the same maker sells a phone with a lower reported footprint at a similar price, it is shown, with what EcoCart does not compare: cameras, battery, size.
  3. Act. Choose it, compare the two side by side, or save it for later. Your choice shows up in your dashboard, and "I bought this" adds it to your purchases.

Give your old phone a second life, not a drawer.

  1. Plan. Answer three questions: does it work, its condition, and what you want for it. EcoCart orders the exchange, resale and recycling routes in your city, and shows what each company's own page says about the cost, what you get and broken devices. From a product page, or from the dashboard if you are not buying anything.
  2. Prepare. Back it up, sign out and reset it, with the maker's own instructions. Set a reminder in your calendar.
  3. Hand over. Mark it done. Send the receipt number to have it verified.

Pick the appliance that uses less electricity, every year.

  1. Know. The BEE stars and yearly kWh the listing states, and what that means in CO₂e a year on India's grid.
  2. Check. Shortlist one, open another: EcoCart compares them only if they are the same type and about the same size. Type the rate from your electricity bill to see what each costs to run a year, and how long a higher price takes to repay.
  3. Act. Choose the one that uses less. The difference is counted per year, apart from one-off footprints.

See the footprint behind everyday buys.

  1. Know. A category estimate from Open CEDA's India data when the category can be matched, always labelled as an estimate. Otherwise the card says why there is none. In search results, every result with a price gets the same estimate as a badge.
  2. Check. Labels the listing claims, such as GOTS or Fairtrade, marked as the seller's claims.
  3. Act. Save it for later, or tap "I bought this" to log it in your purchases. An estimate describes a category, so EcoCart does not use it to pick between two products.

Sources

Unknown means
unknown.

Greener choices need honest numbers. No number is made up by AI or filled in with a guess. Estimates are labelled as estimates, and if a maker has not published something, the card says so.

01

Security updates

Google's Pixel update policy, Apple's UK security statements, Samsung India's announcements, and the per-model support lists published by Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, OnePlus and realme.

02

Lifecycle carbon

Each maker's own environmental report or life cycle assessment, with the storage size or years of use it assumes.

03

Everyday comparisons

India GHG Program road transport factors and the Central Electricity Authority's grid emission factor.

04

Category estimates

Open CEDA 2025 by Watershed: average emissions per rupee for each kind of product in India.

05

Old-device routes

Each company's own page: how the handover works, the cost, what you get, whether broken devices are taken and where, with the date we last checked it. What a page does not say is shown as not stated.

Privacy

What EcoCart keeps,
and what it never sees.

No account. No name, email or phone number is ever sent to us.

Only what it needs. On a product page, the title, category path and price are sent to work out the facts. On a search page, each result's title and price, in one request. None of it is stored.

Anonymous plans. Your plans are tied to a random token. Our servers store only a hash of it.

Your history stays home. The devices you checked stay in your browser. Order pages are never read.

Privacy

EcoCart shows figures published by manufacturers and public bodies and links each one to its source. It does not certify products or recyclers, and completed plans are reported by users. Built on AWS: Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB and Amplify Hosting, deployed with AWS SAM, with agent teams built on the open-source Strands Agents SDK that propose new devices and category rules for a person to approve. Inspired by the 2023 EcoCart project. Written with help from Claude Code and OpenAI Codex.
