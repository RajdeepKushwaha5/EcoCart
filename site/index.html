Use cases

Four moments.
One useful action each.

Each journey ends with something the shopper can do, and the dashboard shows what came of it.

Compare what the makers promise and report.

  1. Know. Security updates promised, and the lifecycle footprint the maker reports, each linked to the official page.
  2. Understand. Where it sits against other phones, and the footprint in kilometres driven. Only one maker's reports are compared.
  3. Act. If the same maker sells a lower-footprint phone at a similar price, choose it. The choice is recorded in your dashboard.

Plan where the old one goes, then track it.

  1. Plan. Pick an exchange, resale or recycling route in your city, taken from each company's own page.
  2. Hand over. Mark it done in the dashboard when it is handed over. The plant grows a stage.
  3. Verify. A handover counts as verified only when the team has seen the receipt or the collector's confirmation.

See a clearly labelled estimate for everyday buys.

  1. Know. A category estimate from Open CEDA's India data, worked out from the price, and labelled as an estimate every time.
  2. Understand. What it is roughly the same as, and where the category sits among 59 product types.
  3. Act. Refill and bigger packs where they apply, and tips that are marked as general.

Spot the labels a listing claims.

  1. Know. GOTS, OEKO-TEX, Fairtrade and other schemes named on the listing, each linked to the scheme.
  2. Understand. They are the seller's claims. EcoCart says so, and does not check them.
  3. Act. Shortlist it, and keep clothes longer: tips that are marked as general.

Privacy

What EcoCart keeps,
and what it never sees.

No account. No name, email or phone number, ever.

Only what it needs. On a product page, the title, category path and price are sent to work out the facts, and are not stored.

Anonymous plans. Your plans are tied to a random token. Our servers store only a hash of it.

Your history stays home. The devices you checked stay in your browser. Order pages are never read.

Privacy

EcoCart shows figures published by manufacturers and public bodies and links each one to its source. It does not certify products or recyclers, and completed plans are reported by users. Built on AWS: Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB and Amplify Hosting, deployed with AWS SAM, with agent teams built on the open-source Strands Agents SDK that propose new devices and category rules for a person to approve. Inspired by the 2023 EcoCart project. Written with help from Claude Code and OpenAI Codex.
