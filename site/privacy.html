EcoCart

Privacy

Last updated 11 October 2026. This covers the EcoCart browser extension and this website.

EcoCart has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email, phone number or address, and it does not use ads, analytics or trackers. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

What the extension sends to the EcoCart server

On a product page on Amazon.in or Flipkart The product title, the shop's category path, the listing's details table and the price. They are used to answer that one request and are not stored. The one exception: when no category rule matches, the category path alone (never the title or anything about you) is counted, so we know which rules to add.
Looking for a renewed version On a device page, the extension may run one search on Amazon.in itself (for example "iPhone 16 renewed") to find a renewed listing of the same model. That request goes to Amazon, as if you had typed the search, and the results stay in your browser.
On a search page The extension downloads the list of covered devices and matches results inside your browser. For the other results, it sends each result's title and price, in one request per page, to get the category estimate shown on its badge. These are not stored or counted, and your search words are never sent.
On an order confirmation page Nothing. The page is not read.
When you save a plan for an old device The device you are buying (its name), the brand and age of the old device, your city, the option you picked, and later whether you marked it done. Plans are linked to a random token, not to you.
When you ask for a handover to be verified The reference you type, such as the collector and a receipt or order number. It is kept with that plan so the team can check it. Please leave out names, addresses and phone numbers.
When you tap "Something looks wrong?" The kind of problem you pick (for example "The price is wrong"), the shop, and the listing's public product ID. Nothing from the page and nothing about you. Reports are counted so the team can fix the reading of that listing.

What is stored, and where

  • On our server (Amazon Web Services, Mumbai region): your saved plans, linked to a one-way hash of a random token. We cannot tell who you are from it. Totals shown on this site (plans saved, marked done and verified) are simple counters. A plan is marked verified only when the EcoCart team has seen a collection receipt or the collector's confirmation; only a short note of what was seen is kept, never personal details.
  • In your browser only: the random token, the devices you recently looked at (for the dashboard and the order-page reminder), device matches you confirmed, the city you last picked, your shortlist, the choices you record between shortlisted products, the reminder dates you set, the products you looked at recently and the purchases you log (name, shop, price and footprint), the first name you give the dashboard, the electricity rate you type on an appliance card, and a list of what you did (product name, kind and date) for the activity chart. None of this leaves your browser, unless you download a backup file yourself.
  • Server logs: errors are logged to help fix problems and deleted after 14 days. They do not contain your plans.

AI

If a product's category cannot be matched by our fixed rules, its title and category path may be sent to a model on Amazon Bedrock, inside the same AWS account, to pick a category from a fixed list. Amazon Bedrock does not use this data to train models. The model never produces any of the numbers shown to you.

Your choices

  • Delete any plan from the EcoCart dashboard. It is removed from the server straight away.
  • Remove shortlisted products and undo choices in the dashboard, and clear the activity history with one button.
  • Removing the extension deletes everything it kept in your browser. Plans left on the server can no longer be linked to anyone.
  • Each product card has a Hide button if you would rather not see it.

Permissions the extension asks for

  • amazon.in and flipkart.com: to read product pages and show the card.
  • The EcoCart API address: to fetch facts and save plans.
  • Storage: for the browser-only data listed above.
